Those decisions affect the first migrations and determine whether cash, stock, and exports can be reconciled after a correction.

## 2. Requirement-to-architecture fit

| Requirement | Fit and change needed |
|---|---|
| Enter a day once | Use a single branch business-day record for manual POS entry, cash entries, Product Mix, stock activity, and export selection. Cashier and storekeeper can enter their respective work against the same day. Capture distinct reported figures and calculate their derived values on the server. |
| Close and reconcile cash | The proposed entities cover the inputs. Add a revisioned close with explicit draft, posting, and correction rules. Morning/evening detail, denomination counts, two independent ledgers, cancellations, and carry-forward belong in the initial design. |

| Installable web app and later kiosk | Use an online-first PWA on laptop, tablet, and phone. Add the dedicated kiosk after the operating workflow is stable. Keep Foodics import gated on a verified export format, and leave direct integration, desktop delivery, and multi-branch rollout outside MVP. |

## 3. Data model review

- **Branch, business day, and identity.** Enforce one business day per branch and local business date. Give operational rows branch-scoped foreign keys so a valid ID from a different branch cannot be linked. Keep Auth identity, employee record, and branch membership distinct. Preserve the cashier, cash counter, and cash counter's role at the time of posting. Store the business date explicitly as a date, and use timestamp-with-time-zone for actual events. Use the configured branch timezone to derive the local order creation date. A Foodics report printed after midnight still belongs to its explicitly stated business date.
- **Financial source of truth.** Store POS revenue, order count, and payment observations with their source. Use separate signed cash and purchasing-custody ledger entries with account/category, effective business date, recorded time, and actor. Give each ledger an approved opening balance at cutover. Derive closing balance, next opening, and monthly summaries in business-date order. A transfer between the two ledgers needs linked debit and credit entries within one transaction if the approved accounting mapping calls for that treatment. Derive counted cash from denomination and quantity lines. Version the reconciliation rules and document the basis for average order value, card ratio, expected cash, variance sign, and rounding. Capture executed bank remittance separately from cash designated for the bank so report mappings can distinguish the two. Include cash-funded expenses in the drawer calculation according to the approved cash ledger mapping. Use decimal money and quantities. Distinguish unknown, explicit zero, and inapplicable values. Link cancellations to the relevant day and preserve the supplied original/replacement identifiers, times, values, reason, and optional customer contact data. Any effect on financial figures needs an approved mapping.
- **Posting and corrections.** Make posted ledger entries and close revisions immutable. A correction should append a linked reversal or adjustment with a reason and approver, then produce a new revision. Define the posting prerequisites for closing versus inventory so missing Product Mix or counts remain visibly incomplete. When an earlier change affects carry-forward, recompute later balances in the current reporting view and retain the original revisions used by prior exports. Flag affected later closes for review under the agreed correction policy.
- **Product Mix and stock.** Keep reported sold quantity and sales-line amount independent of ingredient units and costs. Preserve the reported label/code and the catalog resolution. Store explicit unit conversions and an approved, effective BOM version including yield where supplied. Each consumption movement should identify the Product Mix line, BOM version and component, conversion used, and resulting base-unit quantity. Unmapped lines and missing conversions block affected consumption posting and surface in the review queue. Model opening stock and every receipt, transfer, purchase, return, waste, hospitality, deduction, and approved adjustment as typed movements with source references. A purchase receipt contributes stock once. Derive book stock as of the count, and preserve the observed physical count and resulting variance.
- **Replenishment and receipt.** Persist the suggestion inputs, history/date window, rule version, and output presented for approval. Use the documented calculation of forecast demand plus safety stock less usable stock and confirmed incoming stock. Count only confirmed outstanding quantities eligible for the same planning horizon, with actual receipts already reflected in stock. Surface missing history and configuration explicitly. Capture overrides with original value, changed value, reason, and actor. Link receiving records to order lines so partial receipts have their own dates and quantities. Apply the two-calendar-day default to the branch-local order creation date.

## 4. Security and authorization review

- **Application roles.** Bootstrap Abdo as System Admin through a controlled invitation and assignment. Give Cashier closing drafts, expenses, payment totals, and permitted draft exports. Give Storekeeper stock entry and purchase-order proposals. Grant Manager close and adjustment approval explicitly. Check report export, finalization, stock adjustment, recipe approval, role assignment, and receipt-date override as separate permissions. Keep app-role assignment independent of infrastructure and GitHub access. Restrict cancellation customer contact fields to specifically authorized users and report variants.
- **Database boundary.** Resolve current branch membership and permitted actions from database-owned records keyed to the authenticated identity. Check them in Next.js handlers and enforce them at PostgreSQL entry points. Use explicit grants and RLS on exposed relations, with branch-scoped read and write predicates and RLS-aware report views. Keep ledger and audit tables unavailable for direct client DML. Use narrowly granted transactional commands for posting and corrections. Any privileged command must check the caller, branch, and action in the database and have a fixed search path. Keep Supabase secret/service-role credentials on the server for narrowly scoped administrative work. Ensure role revocation takes effect on the next protected request, including during an existing login session. [Supabase API security](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/api/securing-your-api) · [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security)
- **Audit and files.** Record actor, branch, action, target, business date, recorded time, revision or adjustment reference, and reason where applicable. Commit audit entries with the underlying operation. Make them append-only for application roles. Keep report files private and authorize each download against the current branch and report permission. Keep original workbooks and photographs in the private development repository and outside deployable assets, test fixtures, and application storage. Use anonymized fixtures in automated tests and exclude cancellation contact details and raw operational documents from application logs. ## 5. Reliability review

- **Atomic writes and retries.** For close posting, BOM posting, and receiving, use one database transaction per command, including the affected rows, revision or status change, derived records, and audit. Serialize competing writes on the relevant business day or order and check the expected revision. Scope a unique idempotency key to the branch and action, and associate it with a request fingerprint and the committed result. A retry after a lost response should recover that result. A different payload with the same key or a competing stale revision should return a conflict for review. Enforce uniqueness on posted consumption and receipt source references as a second line of protection. [PostgreSQL transaction isolation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/transaction-iso.html)
- **Exports and recovery.** Pin an export to a consistent set of source revisions before rendering, and record branch, period, reporting variant, rule and template versions, requester, and outcome. A failure during rendering or upload must leave a retryable or failed export record. Expose a download only after the artifact is complete. Reconcile abandoned uploads on retry or cleanup. Set and test recovery point and recovery time targets. Rehearse a database restore and separately test recovery or regeneration of retained files. Supabase database backups cover Storage metadata and omit object bytes. [Supabase database backups](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/backups)
- **Installed app and connectivity.** Cache only static assets. Fetch authenticated pages, API responses, and downloads over the network. Show clear save confirmation and a disconnected state, preserving the user's ability to verify whether a submit succeeded before retrying. Test app install, reconnect, and sign-out on the branch's supported devices. [Next.js PWA guide](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/progressive-web-apps)

## 7. Attendance review

Keep the fixed-camera kiosk outside the MVP release criteria. When the core is stable, run a trial on the chosen device before deciding whether verification needs a separate service. Give the kiosk a revocable, branch-bound identity with permission to submit only attendance events. Bind a verified result to the employee, device, event, and time. Require liveness-aware face verification before accepting a punch. Send failures or exceptions through the approved review process. Capture check-in/out events and retain duplicate or missing punch evidence and manager corrections in an audit trail. Keep face verification data isolated from general employee and operating records, with access, retention, and deletion governed by the approved attendance policy. Use synthetic data for development and keep real biometric material out of Git.

## 10. Production release questions that can stay open in sprint one

1. **Cash policy and cutover.** What are the approved opening balances for cash and purchasing custody, and what historical coverage is required? How are remittance, receipts, expenses, cancellations, and prior-day corrections treated in each ledger and report? Confirm card-ratio denominator, rounding, tolerance, and zero-denominator display.
2. **Branch day and approvals.** What IANA branch timezone and operating-day cutoff apply, including after-midnight work? Which data is required before posting cash or stock, who can approve each action, and how should later closed days be reviewed after a prior-day correction? Who may view or export cancellation contact fields, and for how long are they retained?
3. **Catalog and stock.** Which product codes, add-ons, ingredient and packaging units, recipes, yields, and effective dates are approved? Confirm handling for returns, transfers, waste, hospitality, count adjustments, and opening stock.
4. **Replenishment.** What anchors the three-month view, and which historical dates qualify for the same-weekday comparison and forecast? Confirm the demand horizon, usable stock and incoming eligibility, sparse-history behavior, safety stock, pack sizes, and shelf-life constraints. Which suppliers, cutoffs, or calendar exceptions, if any, change the documented two-calendar-day default?
5. **Report sign-off.** Who supplies the complete approved real-day packet and signs off the exact mapping for the daily ledger, daily statement, and all five families? Confirm weekly versus monthly comparison periods, approved template versions, and the handling of blanks and workbook discrepancies.
6. **Production operations.** Who owns hosting, the domain, application access recovery, and deployment approvals? What retention, backup, recovery, and availability targets will the branch accept for the database and report artifacts?

Use anonymized fixture values and configurable rules in sprint one. A verified Foodics export format is a gate for optional import. Camera device selection, verification thresholds, enrollment and deletion, shifts, breaks, overtime, and correction policy are gates for the later attendance release.
